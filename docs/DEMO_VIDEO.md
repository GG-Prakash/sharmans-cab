# 2-minute demo video: ad click → booking → cost per booking

Record this on your phone's screen recorder (Android: Quick Settings → Screen record, with microphone on), on the live Vercel link, not localhost. Many owners watch only the video, so it has to make sense with the sound off as well: say what is on screen, and let each screen sit for a second.

## Before you press record

1. Open the desk (`/admin`), sign in, and tap **Reset samples** so the sample month is dated to today.
2. Have this "ad link" ready to paste. It's what a Google Ads click looks like:
   `https://sharmans-cab.vercel.app/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=Chennai+Airport+Taxi&utm_term=chennai+airport+taxi&gclid=DEMO-VIDEO`
3. Use your own mobile number in the form. Turn on Do Not Disturb.

## Shot list (about 2:00)

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | Paste the ad link and open it. Hold on the hero. | "This is what a customer sees after tapping your Google ad. Call, WhatsApp or a quick quote form, right at the top." |
| 0:10–0:20 | Scroll slowly to the fleet and the "How fares work" box. | "Your vehicles and your published terms: ₹600 a day driver allowance, 250 km minimum, GST 5%. Prices build trust." |
| 0:20–0:45 | Back to the form: Airport, Chennai Airport → T. Nagar, tomorrow, Sedan, your name and number. Tap **Get my fare**. | "The customer fills this in 20 seconds. They get a reference number and a WhatsApp button." |
| 0:45–1:00 | Switch to the desk. The new lead is at the top, marked New, source **Google Ads**. | "It lands in your booking desk instantly, and it knows it came from Google Ads, which campaign and which keyword." |
| 1:00–1:20 | Open it. Tap **Send / revise quote**, enter ₹1,450, save. | "Your staff calls, then logs the quote. Every step is timed, so you see how fast the desk responds." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Tap **Mark booked**, enter ₹1,400. Show the timeline. | "Customer agrees: booked, with the real value. Nothing gets lost in a notebook or a WhatsApp chat." |
| 1:35–1:55 | Open **Reports**. Type Google Ads spend ₹12,000 and tap **Calculate**. Hold on the **cost per booking** card. | "Now the number that matters: what each *booking* cost you on Google, not each click or form." |
| 1:55–2:00 | Back to the landing page. | "That's the whole loop, from ad to booking to cost. Happy to set it up on your site." |

## After recording

- Open the lead you created and mark it **Booked → Cancelled**, so the test click ID is never exported as a real Google Ads conversion.
- Trim the start and end, then send the video on WhatsApp **with** the link. Keep it under 16 MB so it plays inline (720p is fine).
